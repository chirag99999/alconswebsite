About ALCONS

"We don't decorate buildings. We resolve them — structure, light and material as a single argument, made once and made well."

Founded in 2011 by a small group of architects tired of watching good drawings die in bad handoffs, ALCONS grew into a full-service studio precisely so that wouldn't happen again. Today the same principle holds: the people who design a building are the people accountable for it standing well.

Precision

Tolerances measured in millimetres, not opinions.

Restraint

Every material earns its place before it's specified.

Accountability

One team, start to finish — no handoffs to hide behind.

Questions
How long does a typical project take?

A single residence usually runs 8–14 months from first sketch to handover; larger commercial work is scoped individually during discovery.

Do you work outside Balasore?

Yes — our design team works nationally; construction delivery is currently concentrated across eastern India, with select outstation projects.

Can I hire ALCONS for design only?

Yes. The Essential and Professional tiers can be scoped as design-only engagements if you already have a contractor in place.
